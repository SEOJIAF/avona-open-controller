Find your 3.0-generation cooker

This sets up a Precision Cooker 3.0, Nano 3.0, Mini/Lite or Pro 3.0 the same way the Anova app does.

  • Plug the cooker in and keep it within a few metres of this device.
  • Use Chrome or Edge with Bluetooth turned on.
  • Have your 2.4 GHz Wi‑Fi name and password ready.

Put the cooker in pairing mode (a new cooker usually is already, right after plugging in):

  • Precision Cooker 3.0 / Nano 3.0: press and hold the timer and – icons for 10 seconds.
  • Mini / Lite: press and hold the button for 10 seconds until the LED blinks.
  • Pro 3.0: open the Wi‑Fi menu on the cooker's display.

This browser can't use Bluetooth. Open this page in Chrome or Edge (Android, Windows, macOS or ChromeOS) over HTTPS or localhost.

Mock mode: a simulated cooker answers instead of real Bluetooth, and nothing is sent to Anova.
